Discover Solapur District

Solapur is a major industrial and cultural city in the south-western region of Maharashtra,India, located near the Karnataka border on the Deccan Plateau.

Solapur is a promient district reowned for its deep histroical roots, magnificent temples, and vibrant cultural heritage. situated on the banks of the Sina River, it bridges rich traditions with modern growth.

The district is home to iconic landmarks such as the histroic Bhuikot Fort and the revered Shri Siddeshwar Temple, which stands proudly in the middle of a serene lake.Solpaur is also widely celebrated for its traditional power looms and textile craftsmanship.

Beyond city center, the district boasts other legndary spiritual destinaations, including the famous Vitthal-Rukmini Temple in Pandharpur and the reverd Swami Samrath Maharaj math in Akkalkot, which draw millions of pilgrims every year.
